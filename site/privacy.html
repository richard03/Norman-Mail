Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Norman Mail is an email client for Windows that runs entirely on your computer. This policy explains what data the application accesses, where it is kept and who can see it. In short: your data stays on your computer. The author of Norman Mail does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

1. Data the application accesses

  • Your email: messages (headers, content and attachments) and folders of the accounts you add to the application.
  • Sign-in data: for Gmail accounts connected with Sign in with Google, an OAuth token issued by Google; for other accounts, the password you enter.
  • Your email address (Google scopes openid and email), to identify the account you signed in with.
  • Data the application creates: settings, sorting rules, ToDo and snooze marks, addresses of people you correspond with (for address suggestions) and the trained spam filter.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the features of the email client to you: downloading and showing your messages, searching, sorting them into views, moving, archiving and deleting messages at your request, sending messages you write, suggesting addresses and filtering spam. Norman Mail does not use your data for advertising, does not sell it, does not use it to train AI or machine learning models (the spam filter is trained only locally, on your computer, for your own use), and does not show it to anyone.

3. Where the data is stored

All data is stored only on your computer: messages and settings in the application's data folder (%APPDATA%\Mailer, or the data folder next to the program in portable mode), and passwords and sign-in tokens in Windows Credential Manager. Norman Mail has no servers and no user accounts of its own.

4. Sharing and transfer

Norman Mail does not share or transfer your data to the author or to any third party. The application connects only to:

  • the mail servers of your accounts (for Gmail: Google's IMAP and SMTP servers and Google sign-in), to download and send your mail;
  • GitHub (github.com), once a day, to check for a new version of the application; this request contains only the application version, no data about you or your mail;
  • servers hosting images and other remote content in a message, only when you choose to show that content.

5. Google user data

Data received from Google APIs (your Gmail messages and your email address) is used only to provide the user-facing email client features described above, it is stored only on your computer and it is not transferred to anyone. No person, including the author of Norman Mail, reads your data.

Norman Mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your control and deleting data

  • Removing an account in the application deletes its stored messages and its password or token.
  • To delete all data, close the application and delete its data folder (%APPDATA%\Mailer) and the program file.
  • You can revoke Norman Mail's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Automatic update checks can be turned off in Settings → About.

7. Security

Connections to mail servers are encrypted (TLS). Passwords and tokens are protected by Windows Credential Manager. Messages are shown in an isolated frame where scripts cannot run, and application updates are verified by a digital signature.

8. Children

Norman Mail does not collect data from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: GitHub Issues.

Česky: Zásady ochrany soukromí

Norman Mail je poštovní klient pro Windows, který běží celý ve vašem počítači. Vaše data zůstávají u vás. Autor aplikace žádná vaše data nedostává, neshromažďuje ani neukládá.

  • K čemu má aplikace přístup: ke zprávám a složkám účtů, které do ní přidáte, k přihlašovacímu tokenu Googlu nebo heslu a k vaší e-mailové adrese.
  • K čemu data slouží: jen k funkcím poštovního klienta (stahování, zobrazení, třídění, přesouvání a mazání zpráv na váš pokyn, odesílání, našeptávání adres, filtr spamu). Nepoužívají se k reklamě, neprodávají se a neslouží k trénování AI. Filtr spamu se učí jen ve vašem počítači.
  • Kde jsou uložená: jen ve vašem počítači (složka %APPDATA%\Mailer, hesla a tokeny ve Správci pověření Windows). Aplikace nemá žádné servery.
  • Komu se předávají: nikomu. Aplikace se připojuje jen k poštovním serverům vašich účtů, jednou denně ke GitHubu kvůli kontrole aktualizací (bez jakýchkoli údajů o vás) a k serverům s obrázky ve zprávě, jen když jejich zobrazení povolíte.
  • Data od Googlu se používají jen pro funkce poštovního klienta, ukládají se jen ve vašem počítači a nikomu se nepředávají. Použití a předávání informací získaných z Google API se řídí zásadami Google API Services User Data Policy včetně požadavků Limited Use.
  • Smazání: odebráním účtu v aplikaci se smažou jeho zprávy i heslo nebo token; všechna data smažete odstraněním složky %APPDATA%\Mailer. Přístup k účtu Google zrušíte na myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Kontakt: GitHub Issues.
