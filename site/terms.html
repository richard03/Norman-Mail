Terms of Service

Effective date: 18 November 2026

1. The application

Norman Mail is a free email client for Windows. It runs on your computer and connects directly to the mail servers of the accounts you add. There is no online service and no user account with the author.

2. Use

You may use Norman Mail free of charge for personal and business purposes. You are responsible for the accounts you connect and for the messages you send, and you must follow the terms of your email providers (for Gmail, the Google Terms of Service). Do not use Norman Mail to send spam or for any unlawful purpose.

3. Updates

Norman Mail checks once a day for a new version on GitHub and installs it after a restart. Updates are verified by a digital signature. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings → About.

4. Privacy

How the application handles your data is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: your data stays on your computer.

5. No warranty

Norman Mail is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. Keep in mind that actions you take in the application (for example deleting messages or emptying the trash) are carried out on your mail server. Keep your own backups of important mail.

6. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, the author is not liable for any damage arising from the use of Norman Mail, including loss of data or messages.
